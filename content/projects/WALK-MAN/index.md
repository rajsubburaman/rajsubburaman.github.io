### Description
This video shows a gazebo simulation of the WALK-MAN humanoid opening a sliding door using its arms. The whole-body manipulation trajectory to slide the doors were generated online using the OpenSOT framework. The force-torque sensors installed at the wrist of the arms were used to detect the robot's hand contact with the doors.
